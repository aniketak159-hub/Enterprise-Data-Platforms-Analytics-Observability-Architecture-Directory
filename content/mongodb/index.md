## Core Capabilities

- **MongoDB Enterprise Advanced** — Enterprise sharding, Ops Manager, encryption-at-rest, field-level governance
- **MongoDB Atlas** — Fully managed multi-cloud with vector search (Atlas Search)
- **Cassandra** — Linear scalability, multi-datacenter replication, tunable consistency
- **DataStax** — Enterprise Cassandra with Kaskada real-time streaming for AI
- **Vector Search** — Native vector indexing for RAG/GenAI workloads
- **Data Sovereignty** — In-country deployment for KSA/UAE regulations
