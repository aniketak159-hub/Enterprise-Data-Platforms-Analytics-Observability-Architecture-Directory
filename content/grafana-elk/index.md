## Core Capabilities

### Elastic Stack (ELK)
- **Elasticsearch** — Distributed search & analytics engine
- **Logstash** — Data processing pipelines for ingestion
- **Kibana** — Visualization & dashboarding
- **SIEM** — Security analytics, threat detection, compliance reporting

### Grafana Enterprise LGTM Stack
- **Loki** — Log aggregation (cost-effective, labels-based)
- **Mimir** — Long-term metrics storage (Prometheus-compatible)
- **Tempo** — Distributed tracing backend
- **Grafana** — Unified visualization with RBAC & audit logging

---

## Compliance Alignment

- **CERT-In** — Mandatory central log retention (India)
- **RBI/SEBI** — Financial sector audit logging
- **SAMA/NCA ECC** — KSA critical infrastructure
- **GDPR/DPDPA** — Data privacy & consent auditability
