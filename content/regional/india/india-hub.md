## 🛡️ Compliance Frameworks

<div align="center">

| Framework | Authority | Scope | Key Requirements |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **RBI Cyber Security Framework** | Reserve Bank of India | Banking & Financial Services | In-country data residency, HSM encryption keys, financial telemetry protection, audit logging |
| **SEBI Cloud Computing Guidelines** | Securities & Exchange Board of India | Capital Markets | Strict audit logging, access governance, cloud risk management, regulatory inspection readiness |
| **DPDPA** | MeitY | All Sectors | Data minimization, consent management audit trails, privacy-by-design architectures |
| **CERT-In Cyber Security Directions** | CERT-In | All Entities | Mandatory central log retention (180 days), 6-hour incident reporting, NTP synchronization |
| **MeitY Guidelines** | Ministry of Electronics & IT | Government Cloud | Empanelled CSPs, data sovereignty, security audits |

</div>
