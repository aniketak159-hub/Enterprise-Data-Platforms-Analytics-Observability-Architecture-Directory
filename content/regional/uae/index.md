## Compliance Frameworks

| Framework | Scope | Key Requirements |
|-----------|-------|------------------|
| **CBUAE** | UAE Central Bank Directives | Financial sector cloud security, data residency, audit trails |
| **PDPL** | Federal Decree-Law No. 45 (Personal Data Protection) | Consent, data minimization, DPIA, cross-border transfers |
| **Dubai Data Law** | Law No. 26 of 2015 | Data sharing, open data, government data classification |
| **DESC** | Dubai Electronic Security Center | Critical infrastructure protection, cloud security |
| **ADGM/DIFC** | Financial Free Zone Regulations | Independent data protection regimes, financial services |
